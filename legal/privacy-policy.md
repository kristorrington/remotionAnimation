# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 August 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Kris Torrington ("I", "me", "my", or "we")
collects, uses, and protects information when you visit **kristorrington.com**
(the "Site") or interact with my content and channels. By using the Site, you
agree to the practices described here.

## 1. Who I am

I am an independent content creator publishing videos and written commentary
about artificial intelligence and technology. You can reach me at
**kris@kristorrington.com**.

## 2. Information I collect

**Information you give me directly:** if you contact me, subscribe to a
newsletter, join a community, or comment, I may collect your name, email
address, and the contents of your message.

**Information collected automatically:** like most websites, my Site and its
hosting/analytics providers may automatically collect standard technical data —
IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, pages viewed, and
the date/time of your visit — via cookies and similar technologies.

**Embedded content:** pages may embed content from third parties (e.g. YouTube
videos). Those embeds can collect data about you as if you had visited the
third party directly.

I do **not** knowingly collect sensitive personal information, and I do not sell
your personal information.

## 3. How I use information

- To operate, maintain, and improve the Site and my content;
- To respond to your enquiries and messages;
- To send updates or newsletters **if** you have opted in (you can unsubscribe
  at any time);
- To understand aggregate audience trends via analytics;
- To comply with legal obligations and protect against misuse.

## 4. Cookies and analytics

The Site may use cookies and analytics tools (for example, privacy-respecting
website analytics) to understand how visitors use the Site. You can control or
disable cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work
correctly if you do.

## 5. Third-party services

I rely on reputable third parties that have their own privacy policies,
including (as applicable): website hosting and analytics providers; email/
newsletter services; and the social platforms where I publish (YouTube/Google,
TikTok, and Instagram/Meta). Any automation I use to publish **my own** videos
to **my own** channels accesses only my accounts under those platforms' APIs and
does not collect data about you.

## 6. How information is shared

I do not sell or rent your personal information. I may share limited data with
service providers who help operate the Site (bound by confidentiality), or where
required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

## 7. Data retention

I keep personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes above or
as required by law, then delete or anonymise it.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. under the Australian Privacy Principles, the
EU/UK GDPR, or the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or
restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing.
To exercise any of these, email **kris@kristorrington.com** and I will respond
within a reasonable time.

## 9. Children's privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
jurisdiction), and I do not knowingly collect their personal information.

## 10. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information,
but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## 11. International transfers

If you access the Site from outside Australia, your information may be processed
in Australia or other countries where my service providers operate, which may
have different data-protection laws.

## 12. Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows
the latest revision; material changes will be posted on this page.

## 13. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email **kris@kristorrington.com**.
